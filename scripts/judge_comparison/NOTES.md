# Results: Judge Comparison

Rows are datasets, columns are judge × method (BT = BradleyTerry, DJ = Direct Judge / answer matching without ground truth).

## Key findings

- **Pairwise aggregation (BT) works with weak judges**: on three models (phi-4 and the gpt-oss models) BT substantially outperforms Direct Judge. Pairwise comparison is an easier task than absolute answer grading.
- **Strong judges close the gap**: for o3 and gpt-oss-120b, Direct Judge is competitive with or better than BT. o3 DJ achieves the best overall performance.
- **phi-4 Direct Judge is near-random**: mean tau ~0.03, mean r ~-0.01 — the model cannot reliably grade open-ended answers without ground truth. BT with phi-4 is much more robust (mean tau ~0.60).
- **gemma-3-27b-it** reverses weak judge pattern. BT τ = 0.24 vs. DJ τ = 0.67 on SimpleQA
